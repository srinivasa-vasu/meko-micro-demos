# LEND-UW-09: Unsecured personal loans to self-employed borrowers (underwriting policy)

Fictional demo policy for the Meko GOAP demo. Not a lending, credit, regulatory or legal policy.
Owner: Credit Risk. Applies to: credit underwriters and agents underwriting unsecured personal loans.

## LEND-UW-09 policy: scope

Use this policy only for self-employed borrowers: sole traders, contractors and owners of a business who pay
themselves from it. Their income is not shown on an employer's pay stubs.

## LEND-UW-09 policy: verification and thresholds

1. Income: two years of tax returns and year-to-date profit and loss statements, averaged.
2. Business: proof the business has traded for at least two years.
3. Credit score of at least 700; debt-to-income (DTI) up to 36% approve; above 36% decline.
