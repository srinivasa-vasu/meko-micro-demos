# CARD-DISPUTE-03: Customer-initiated chargeback (dispute policy)

Fictional demo policy for the Meko GOAP demo. Not a banking, card-network, regulatory or legal policy.
Owner: Payments Operations. Applies to: payments specialists and agents handling card disputes.

## CARD-DISPUTE-03 policy: scope

Use this policy only when the customer has raised a chargeback through their card issuer. The issuer, not the
merchant, is then running the dispute.

## CARD-DISPUTE-03 policy: handling

1. Follow the card network's chargeback process and its deadlines.
2. Do not issue a provisional refund from our side while the chargeback is open, to avoid refunding twice.
3. Submit the authorization record, the capture record and the customer correspondence as evidence.
