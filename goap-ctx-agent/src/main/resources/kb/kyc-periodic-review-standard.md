# KYC-STD-04: KYC periodic re-review standard

Fictional demo standard for the Meko GOAP demo. Not regulatory guidance. Owner: Financial Crime Compliance. Applies to
periodic KYC re-reviews of business customers.

## KYC-STD-04 compare, don't restart

A periodic KYC re-review compares the current profile against the previous assessment of the same customer. Use the
latest review of exactly that customer id, not an older review and not a similar customer.

## KYC-STD-04 what needs new evidence

- Unchanged registered address and ownership need no new evidence.
- A newly added beneficiary is a material change: request targeted source-of-funds evidence and sanctions screening
  for that beneficiary, and require a human reviewer's sign-off.
- A material increase in expected monthly volume needs an explanation from the customer: monthly volume more than 25%
  outside the previous band is material.

## KYC-STD-04 when there is no prior review

Without a prior assessment to compare against, the KYC review must re-verify identity, address, ownership, source of
funds and every beneficiary.
