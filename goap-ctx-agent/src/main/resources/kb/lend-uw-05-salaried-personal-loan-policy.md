# LEND-UW-05: Unsecured personal loans to salaried borrowers (underwriting policy)

Fictional demo policy for the Meko GOAP demo. Not a lending, credit, regulatory or legal policy.
Owner: Credit Risk. Applies to: credit underwriters and agents underwriting unsecured personal loans.

## LEND-UW-05 policy: scope

Use this policy for unsecured personal loans up to 50,000 USD to salaried borrowers: income paid by an employer and
shown on pay stubs. Self-employed borrowers are underwritten under LEND-UW-09 instead.

## LEND-UW-05 policy: verification

1. Income: two recent pay stubs, confirmed against the stated monthly income.
2. Employment: a verification of employment (VOE) with the employer, completed within 10 days of the credit call.
3. Assets: two months of bank statements, showing the reserves the borrower holds.
4. Credit: one credit report per application, pulled with the borrower's consent. A second pull is a second hard
   inquiry on the borrower's credit file: it needs fresh consent and is recorded on the application.
5. Verifications the loan-origination system already completed are reused, not repeated.

## LEND-UW-05 policy: conditions

1. A single deposit larger than 25% of monthly income must be sourced: a letter of explanation and proof of the source.
2. An employment gap longer than 30 days in the last two years needs a letter of explanation.
3. A condition is cleared on the explanation and its documented evidence. Where the borrower already gave both during
   origination, the condition is cleared from that record; the borrower is not asked again.
4. Acceptable evidence of a deposit's source: a gift letter, or a statement of the account the money came from.
   Acceptable evidence for an employment gap: the employer's letter. Any other evidence needs a ruling from credit risk;
   a ruling applies to later applications with the same kind of evidence.

## LEND-UW-05 policy: credit thresholds

1. Credit score of at least 680.
2. Debt-to-income (DTI), including the new loan's monthly instalment: up to 40% approve; above 40% and up to 45%
   approve only with at least 3 months of reserves; above 45% decline.
3. Record the thresholds applied and the figures they were applied to, so the credit call can be checked later.
