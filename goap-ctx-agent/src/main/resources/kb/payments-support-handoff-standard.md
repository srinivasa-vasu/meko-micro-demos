# SUPPORT-STD-02: Payments support handoff standard

Fictional demo standard for the Meko GOAP demo. Owner: Customer Support. Applies to every handoff from first-line
support (people, chatbots, vendor tools) to a payments specialist.

## SUPPORT-STD-02 what a handoff must carry

- The transaction id and amount.
- What the customer already confirmed (for example: the card is still in their possession).
- The troubleshooting already done, and any hypothesis already rejected.
- The customer's own words for the problem.

## SUPPORT-STD-02 why

A customer must never be asked to repeat themselves. Re-asking is the moment trust breaks: the specialist should
start from what first-line support established, not from the ticket title.
