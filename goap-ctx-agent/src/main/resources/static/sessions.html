New session. Same context.

Every AI harness keeps context inside the session. Close it, switch tools, or hand the work to a teammate, and the next session starts empty: you explain the stack, the decisions and the constraints again. Connect the harness to a Meko datapack, and the next session, in any harness, starts with what the last one established.

The harness. A minimal agent session built into this page, standing in for any real harness (Claude Code, Codex, a support copilot): its context lives in the session and goes when the session ends. What you type is yours; the facts are what Meko extracts from it; what a new session knows is what Meko returns. checking…

2End the session

5Try it in your own harness

The proof that matters is your own tool.
